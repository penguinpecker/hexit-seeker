Brand exploration // 3 directions

Hexit logo concepts

Three directions for the honeycomb tap-trading app. Each one keeps the Kaboom world (deep purple, magenta fuse, amber armed tile) and is shown as a full lockup, an app icon at store and home-screen sizes, and on a light background.

My pick
Hexit

01 · The Exit

A hexagon with its right side blown open, and the price fuse escaping through the gap with a spark at the tip. "Hex" plus "exit" in a single shape, and the motion reads as a price breaking out. It's the simplest of the three and holds up at tiny sizes.

02 · Hex Bomb

The Kaboom bomb, rebuilt as a hexagon: the same ice-blue rim, glossy shine and lit wick, with a magenta "x" (for multiplier) on its face. In the wordmark the dot of the "i" is the spark. It's the most playful direction and the closest relative of the Kaboom brand.

03 · Comb Arrow

Three honeycomb tiles locked together into a right-pointing arrow. The leading tile is the amber armed hex from the game board, with a play chevron and a magenta spark. It reads as "go" and "exit" at once and is built from the real board geometry. It's the cleanest direction and could fit a fintech brand.
